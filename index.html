Privacy Policy

How the game handles your information

At a glance: Color Flow saves your game progress on your device. The game may show ads through Google AdMob. We do not run an account system or a server that receives your game progress.

Effective date: 14 October 2026

This policy covers the Color Flow mobile game for Android and iOS, published by Shazaib Sarwar (“we”, “us”).

Information stored on your device

The game stores progress and preferences locally, including levels, coins, boosters, settings, and reward history. It may also keep recent error details and diagnostic breadcrumbs locally to help diagnose a problem if you contact support. This information is not automatically uploaded to us.

The game has no account, sign-in, chat, or developer-operated analytics service in its standard release configuration.

Advertising

Color Flow uses Google AdMob for interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP address, interactions with the app and ads, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. An IP address may be used to estimate general location. The exact data depends on your device, region, settings, and ad configuration.

Google explains its practices in the Google Privacy Policy and Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Consent and choices

Where required, Google’s consent form is shown before ads are requested. Your choice may affect the ads you see. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s privacy settings. Rewarded ads are optional; you can keep playing without watching one.

Purchases and notifications

Some releases may offer optional in-app purchases. If enabled, purchases are handled by Google Play or Apple’s App Store under their own privacy policies. We do not receive payment card details. Purchases are disabled in the game’s default build configuration.

If you enable notifications, the game may schedule local reminders, such as hearts refilling or daily rewards. You can turn them off in the game or in your device settings.

Sharing, retention, and deletion

We do not sell your personal information. Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. Google and the app stores handle information they collect under their own retention and deletion policies. For that information, use their privacy controls or contact them directly.

Children

Color Flow is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account or developer-operated server. If you have a concern, contact us at the address below.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The current version and effective date will appear here.
